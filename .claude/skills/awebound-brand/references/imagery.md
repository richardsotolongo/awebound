# Illustration and photography

Awebound art looks engraved: fine hatched linework, symmetric monumental compositions, at most two or three inks on a washed base.

## Illustration

- Engraving or etching style with crosshatched shading; no flat clip art, 3D renders or photo collage.
- One central subject, centered and symmetric, framed by architecture, botanicals or chains.
- Two inks plus the garment color by default; accent ink stays sparse.
- The wordmark sits whole above or below the scene, never behind it or cut by it.
- Scripture reference at the base, small, between short rules.
