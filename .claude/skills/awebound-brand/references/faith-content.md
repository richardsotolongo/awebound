# Scripture and faith content

Awebound cites Scripture by reference and lets the art carry the meaning; imagery is symbols only, never a figure of Christ.

## Reference format

- Full book name, chapter, colon, verse; an en dash for ranges: HEBREWS 4:16, HEBREWS 2:14–15, LUKE 24:1–6. No abbreviations.
- Caps on garments; on screen use `ScriptureRef` (Cinzel, `scripture` color, short rules either side).
- Garments carry the reference only. Product pages may add one plain paraphrase line.
- Full verse quotes use the New Living Translation, credited "(NLT)" after the reference. Check Tyndale's NLT permission terms before printing verse text on garments.

## Depiction: symbols only

| Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- |
| The Thorn Cross and other empty crosses | Any depiction of Jesus: face, silhouette, hands, crucifix figure |
| Crown of thorns, thorn vines | God the Father or the Holy Spirit as a person |
| Broken chains, falling links | Gore, wounds, blood for shock |
| The empty tomb, the rolled stone, dawn light | Skulls, inverted crosses, occult or satanic symbols |
| Lilies, olive branches, arches, ruins | Scripture as a joke, pun or unrelated slogan |

Reverent and confident; never mock other believers or traditions, never sell with fear or guilt.
