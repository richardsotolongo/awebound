# Brand and voice

Awebound is an independent Christian apparel brand for believers aged 18 to 30 who want to express their faith boldly. The range covers tees, oversized tees, tank tops and hats, with shorts, bags and more to come. Never describe the whole brand as one cut or garment type.

Awebound sounds reverent, weighty and sure of itself: short sentences, concrete images from Scripture, no sermons.

## Pillars

| Pillar | What it carries | Collection family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Royal heritage | The throne of grace, a kingdom that cannot be shaken, ornament and arches | Royal Heritage (A3-R) |
| Freedom | Chains broken, death without dominion, standing firm | Broken Bond (A3-B) |
| Resurrection | The stone rolled away, the empty tomb at dawn, new life | Rolled Away (A3-T) |

The clothes come first: people should want the piece for its design and quality before they read the verse.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "The stone was rolled away. Wear it like you believe it." | "Blessed vibes only!!" |
| Name the image: chains, thorns, the empty tomb, the throne | Vague uplift: "faith-inspired positivity" |
| Let the reference speak: "Galatians 5:1" | Preach or explain the verse at length |
| Talk about the garment plainly: weight, wash, fit, print | Hype words: "insane", "fire", "drip" |
| Invite: "Stand firm." | Shame or fear: "Don't be lukewarm." |

## Mechanics

- Sentence case in copy and buttons (buttons display caps through styling). No exclamation marks.
- Emoji only in social captions, at most one, never in place of a word.

## Product description template

```
Front: [placement and mark]
Back: [composition in one line]
Base: [garment color] · Inks: [ink colors]
Scripture: [BOOK C:V] · [one-line plain paraphrase]
Fit: [cut for this product: regular, oversized, tank, cap...] · [fabric or material once confirmed]
```

## Naming

- Collections: Royal Heritage, Broken Bond, Rolled Away.
- Product names: two or three words from the art or the verse (Freed in Awe, Third Morning, Shattered Dominion).
- IDs keep the lookbook system: identity, family letter, number (A3-B01; hats use H). IDs show in `accent-text`.

## Calls to action

Shop the collection · Shop [family] · Read the lookbook · Notify me · Add to bag. Avoid "Buy now" and countdown pressure.
